## 🎯 English Compulsory Strategy: "Precision in Writing, Depth in Literature, Flawless Grammar"

Key scoring pillars for 12th English:
1. **Reading Comprehension (15 Marks):** Unseen passages (factual and discursive) with exact line citations, vocabulary in context, and structured note-making with standard abbreviations and indentation.
2. **Writing Skills (15 Marks):** Standard formats for Notice (in a neat box with issuing authority, date, catch-heading), Formal/Informal Invitations & Replies, Report Writing (Headline, By-line, place, chronological event description), and Official Letters / Job Applications with formal Bio-data/CV.
3. **Advanced Grammar (8 Marks):** High-accuracy transformations for Clauses (Noun, Adverb, Relative), Synthesis (Simple, Complex, Compound sentences), Phrasal Verbs (break, bring, carry, come, get, give, go, put, run, turn), and Prepositions of motion, time, and space.
4. **Flamingo & Vistas Literature (42 Marks):** Deep thematic analysis, character sketches (M. Hamel, Saheb, Mukesh, Douglas, Edla Willmansson, Gandhi, Dr. Sadao, Derry, Mr. Lamb), poetic devices (metaphor, simile, personification, imagery), and exact stanza explanations with reference to context.

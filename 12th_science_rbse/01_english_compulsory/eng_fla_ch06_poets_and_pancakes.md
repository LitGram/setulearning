## 🌟 1. Overview & Strategy

> **Teacher's Direct Advice:**  
> Asokamitran's humorous, affectionate reminiscence of Gemini Studios (founded in 1940 in Chennai by S.S. Vasan):  
> 1. **Pancakes:** The brand name of the makeup material bought by truckloads (used by Greta Garbo, Vyjayantimala). The makeup department symbolized national integration (Bengali $\to$ Maharashtrian $\to$ Dharwar Kannadiga $\to$ Andhraite $\to$ Madras Indian Christian).  
> 2. **Kothamangalam Subbu (No. 2 at Gemini):** Flamboyant, multi-talented, loyal, wrote genuine poetry and novels, improvised scenes instantly, charitable yet hated by the office boy.  
> 3. **The Legal Adviser / Lawyer:** Known ironically as the exact opposite of a lawyer; unintentionally ended the career of a talented young actress by secretly recording her backstage tirade.  
> 4. **Moral Re-Armament (MRA) Army & Stephen Spender:** Frank Buchman's MRA (200 people, 20 nationalities) presented two anti-communist plays (*Jotham Valley* and *The Forgotten Factor*). English poet Stephen Spender visited Gemini Studios; his accent baffled the staff until years later the author read *The God That Failed*.

## 🌟 1. Key Preposition Rules & Distinctions

1. **Between vs Among:**  
   - *Between:* For two persons/things (*Divide the apples between Ram and Shyam*).  
   - *Among:* For more than two (*Distribute sweets among the children*).
2. **In vs Into:**  
   - *In:* Static state/inside (*He is in the room*).  
   - *Into:* Dynamic motion from outside to inside (*He jumped into the river*).
3. **Since vs For:**  
   - *Since:* Point of time (*since 2010, since morning*).  
   - *For:* Period/duration of time (*for 5 years, for two hours*).
4. **Fixed Prepositions (Board Favorites):**  
   - *Accused of*, *Addicted to*, *Afraid of*, *Congratulate on*, *Deprived of*, *Fond of*, *Interested in*, *Junior/Senior to*, *Prevent from*, *Tired of*.

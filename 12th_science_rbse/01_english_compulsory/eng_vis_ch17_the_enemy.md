## 🌟 1. Overview & Strategy

> **Teacher's Direct Advice:**  
> Pearl S. Buck's masterly tale set during WWII exploring human compassion rising above nationalistic hatred:  
> 1. **The Dilemma:** Dr. Sadao Hoki and his wife Hana find a wounded American prisoner of war (Tom, a sailor from US Navy) washed ashore near their beach house. Surrendering him means certain death; harboring him is treason.  
> 2. **The Doctor's Oath:** Sadao prioritizes his medical ethics and humanitarian duty. Despite the servants revolting and walking out (Yumi, gardener, cook), Sadao and Hana operate, extract the bullet, and nurse the enemy back to life.  
> 3. **The General's Selfishness:** Sadao truthfully informs the old General Takima, who promises to send private assassins. The General forgets due to his own health anxieties.  
> 4. **The Gentle Escape:** Dr. Sadao provides Tom with a boat, food, water, flashlight, and Japanese clothes to escape to a nearby uninhabited island, wondering: *"Why I could not kill him?"*

## ⚡ 8. 1-Minute Quick Recap
- Protagonists: Dr. Sadao Hoki & Hana (met in America, married in traditional Japanese style)
- Enemy: Tom, American POW (Navy sailor)
- Conflict: Professional Hippocratic oath vs Japanese patriotism
- Climax: General forgets assassins; Sadao helps the enemy escape safely

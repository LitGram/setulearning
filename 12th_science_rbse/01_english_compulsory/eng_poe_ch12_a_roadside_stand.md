## 🌟 1. Overview & Strategy

> **Teacher's Direct Advice:**  
> Robert Frost presents the poignant plea of rural folk who have set up a roadside stand:  
> 1. **Not Begging for Bread:** They are not begging for a dole of bread, but asking for a share of "city money" to elevate their living standard.  
> 2. **Selfish City Travellers:** Passing cars rush by with eyes straight ahead; if they stop, it is only to complain about the signboards (N turned wrong), to turn the car, or to ask for gas.  
> 3. **The 'Greedy Good-Doers':** Corrupt politicians and pseudo-philanthropists who promise to relocate rural folk to village clusters, ostensibly to soothe them, but actually robbing them of independent thought and self-reliance.  
> 4. **Poet's Agony:** Frost laments the childlike longing in vain, wishing for an end to their misery, but realizing compassion and self-help are the true answers.

## ⚡ 8. 1-Minute Quick Recap
- Roadside stand sells: Wild berries in wooden quarts, crook-necked squash
- City folk attitude: Indifferent, annoyed, asking for directions or gas
- 'Greedy good-doers': Deceptive benefactors exploiting the poor
- Core issue: Rural deprivation vs Urban callous indifference

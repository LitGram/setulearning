## 🌟 1. Overview & Strategy

> **Teacher's Direct Advice:**  
> Tishani Doshi's travelogue of Antarctica aboard the Russian vessel *Akademik Shokalskiy*:  
> 1. **Gondwana Supercontinent:** 650 million years ago, a giant southern supercontinent existed with a warm climate and rich flora/fauna before breaking apart into modern continents.  
> 2. **Why Antarctica?** Holds 90% of Earth's total ice volumes, has no human trees or buildings, contains half-million-year-old carbon records trapped in ice cores, and reflects climate change clearly.  
> 3. **Students on Ice Programme:** Initiated by Canadian Geoff Green to take high school students to the end of the Earth to instill respect for the planet. *"Take care of the small things and the big things will take care of themselves"* (e.g. microscopic phytoplankton sustaining marine life).

## 🌟 1. Overview & Strategy

> **Teacher's Direct Advice:**  
> Kalki's brilliant satire on autocratic vanity and the inevitability of destiny:  
> 1. **The Prophecy:** Chief astrologer predicts that the 10-day-old prince of Pratibandapuram will die by a tiger because he was born under the hour of the Bull (Bull and Tiger are natural enemies). The infant miraculous speaks: *"Let tigers beware!"*  
> 2. **The Obsessive Hunt:** Becoming Maharaja Sir Jilani Jung Jung Bahadur, he bans tiger hunting in his state, kills 70 tigers, marries a princess whose father's kingdom has rich tiger forests to hunt 29 more.  
> 3. **The 100th Tiger Farce:** The Dewan brings an old, weak tiger from People's Park in Madras. The King shoots, but the tiger only faints from the shock; hunters kill it in secret to keep their jobs.  
> 4. **The Supreme Irony:** On his son's 3rd birthday, the King buys a cheap wooden toy tiger. A tiny wooden sliver pierces his right hand; infection spreads, and three famous surgeons operate, declaring: *"The operation was successful. The Maharaja is dead."* The 100th tiger took its revenge!

## ⚡ 8. 1-Minute Quick Recap
- Prophecy: 100th tiger will cause death
- King's title: Tiger King (Maharaja of Pratibandapuram)
- British Officer (Durai) bribe: 50 expensive diamond rings to Duraisani (Cost: 3 lakh rupees)
- Ultimate irony: Killed by an inanimate, crude wooden toy tiger

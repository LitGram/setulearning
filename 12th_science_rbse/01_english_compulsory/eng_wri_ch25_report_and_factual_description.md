## 🌟 1. Standard Report Writing Structure

1. **Headline:** Catchy, bold, present tense or passive (e.g. *BLOOD DONATION CAMP AT GSSS MUKLAWA*).  
2. **By-line:** By [Name], Special Correspondent / Student Reporter.  
3. **Dateline:** Place and Date (e.g. *Raisinghnagar, 15 October*).  
4. **Body (Past Tense Only):**  
   - *Opening Paragraph:* What, when, where, organized by whom, and inaugurated by whom.  
   - *Middle Paragraph:* Key activities, speeches, cultural performances, statistics.  
   - *Concluding Paragraph:* Vote of thanks, principal's address, overall outcome.

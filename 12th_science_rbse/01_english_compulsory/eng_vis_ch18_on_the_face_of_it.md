## 🌟 1. Overview & Strategy

> **Teacher's Direct Advice:**  
> A touching one-act play by Susan Hill on physical disability and psychological loneliness:  
> 1. **Derry (14-year-old boy):** Half his face is burnt by acid. Bitter, hypersensitive, withdrawn, believing everyone is terrified of his monstrous appearance.  
> 2. **Mr. Lamb (Old Man):** Lost a leg in the war (now has a tin leg called "Lamey-Lamb"). Lives alone in a big house with an open garden; cheerful, optimistic, welcoming everyone.  
> 3. **Philosophical Lessons:** Weed vs Flower (both are green growing things); Beauty and the Beast; living life fully rather than hiding in fear.  
> 4. **Tragic Climax:** Derry breaks away from his overprotective mother and returns to the garden, only to find Mr. Lamb has fallen from his ladder while picking crab apples and died. But Derry's outlook on life is permanently transformed.

---

## ⚡ 8. 1-Minute Quick Recap
- Derry: Acid-burnt face, deeply cynical and self-conscious
- Mr. Lamb: Tin leg, loves people, makes apple jelly, keeps gates open
- Transformation: Mr. Lamb teaches Derry to look at the world, not just his scars
- Climax: Mr. Lamb dies, but Derry sheds his emotional paralysis

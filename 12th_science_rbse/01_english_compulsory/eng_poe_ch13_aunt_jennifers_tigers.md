## 🌟 1. Overview & Strategy

> **Teacher's Direct Advice:**  
> Adrienne Rich's powerful feminist critique of patriarchal oppression:  
> 1. **The Tigers in the Panel:** Bright topaz denizens of a green world; proud, fearless, pacing in sleek chivalric certainty. They do not fear the men beneath the tree.  
> 2. **Aunt Jennifer's Frailty:** Her fingers are fluttering through her wool; she finds even the ivory needle hard to pull because of the *"massive weight of Uncle's wedding band"* sitting heavily upon her hand.  
> 3. **The Irony & Immortality of Art:** Even after Aunt Jennifer dies with her terrified hands still ringed with ordeals she was mastered by, the tigers she embroidered will go on prancing, proud and unafraid. Art immortalizes the freedom she was denied in life.

---

## ⚡ 8. 1-Minute Quick Recap
- Tigers: Fearless, proud, 'sleek chivalric certainty' (symbol of courage)
- Uncle's wedding band: Symbol of male domination and marital bondage
- Fluttering fingers: Physical and emotional trauma of oppression
- Climax: Aunt dies repressed, but her art remains triumphantly free

## 🌟 1. Overview & Strategy

> **Teacher's Direct Advice:**  
> A.R. Barton explores adolescent fantasizing, hero-worship, and socioeconomic realities among working-class teenagers:  
> 1. **Sophie vs Jansie:** Sophie lives in an imaginative dream world (wants to open a boutique, become an actress or fashion designer); Jansie is sensible, grounded in reality, knowing they are both earmarked for the local biscuit factory.  
> 2. **Geoff (The Brother):** An apprentice mechanic, silent, secretive; Sophie confides her wildest fantasies to Geoff to win his attention.  
> 3. **The Danny Casey Fantasy:** Sophie fabricates a chance meeting with young Irish football prodigy Danny Casey at Royce's window, claiming he promised to meet her by the canal. She waits in vain, confronting the pain of self-delusion.

---

## ⚡ 8. 1-Minute Quick Recap
- Sophie: Daydreamer, escapist; Jansie: Realistic, pragmatic
- Destination: Biscuit factory (working-class background)
- Hero-worship idol: Danny Casey (Irish football star)
- Canal waiting scene: Painful collision of fantasy and harsh reality

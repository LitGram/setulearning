## 🌟 1. Three Golden Patterns of Synthesis

1. **Combining into a Simple Sentence (Only ONE finite verb):**
   - Using Participles (*Seeing the tiger, he ran away.*)
   - Using Infinitives (*He works hard to pass the exam.*)
   - Using Preposition with Gerund (*Besides making a promise, he kept it.*)
   - Using 'Too...to' (*He is too weak to walk.*)

2. **Combining into a Complex Sentence (One Principal + Subordinate Clauses):**
   - Using *though/although*, *because*, *if*, *as soon as*, *so...that*.
   - *He is poor. He is honest.* $\implies$ **Though he is poor, he is honest.**

3. **Combining into a Compound Sentence (Coordinating Conjunctions: FANBOYS):**
   - *and, but, yet, or, so, neither...nor, either...or, not only...but also*.
   - *He worked hard. He failed.* $\implies$ **He worked hard, but he failed.**

## 📝 4. Solved Board Models
1. *He heard the noise. He woke up.* (Use Participle)  
   $\implies$ **Hearing the noise, he woke up.**  
2. *He is very old. He cannot run fast.* (Use 'too...to')  
   $\implies$ **He is too old to run fast.**

# English Chapter 26: Letter Writing & Job Application — Smart Study Guide

## 🌟 1. Job Application with Bio-data (Master Template)

```
Sender's Address
Date

The Principal / The Director
Institution Name, City

Subject: Application for the post of [Post Name, e.g. PGT English]

Sir/Madam,
In response to your advertisement in 'The Rajasthan Patrika' dated [Date], 
I wish to offer my candidature for the post of [Post Name] in your prestigious institution.

I possess the requisite qualifications and experience. I am hardworking, dedicated, 
and possess excellent communication skills. My detailed Bio-data is enclosed herewith for your kind perusal.

Yours faithfully,
[Signature]
[Full Name]

Encl.: Bio-data & Testimonials

----------------------------- BIO-DATA -----------------------------
1. Name: [Candidate Name]
2. Father's Name: [Father's Name]
3. Date of Birth: [DD/MM/YYYY]
4. Address: [Full Postal Address]
5. Contact: [Phone & Email]
6. Educational Qualifications:
   - 10th: RBSE (88%)
   - 12th: RBSE (91%)
   - B.A. (Hons): University of Rajasthan (76%)
   - M.A. English: University of Rajasthan (74%)
   - B.Ed.: Govind Guru Tribal University (82%)
7. Experience: 3 Years as PGT English at ABC Senior Secondary School
8. References:
   (i) Dr. R.K. Sharma, Principal, ABC School
   (ii) Prof. S.K. Verma, Head of Dept., University of Rajasthan
--------------------------------------------------------------------
```

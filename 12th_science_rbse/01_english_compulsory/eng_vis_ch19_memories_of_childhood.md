## 🌟 1. Overview & Strategy

> **Teacher's Direct Advice:**  
> Two autobiographical accounts of marginalization and rebellion against oppression:  
> 1. **Part I: The Cutting of My Long Hair (Zitkala-Sa):**  
>    - Native American girl at Carlisle Indian School.  
>    - Humiliation of cultural assimilation: tight-fitting clothes, eating by formula bells, and the forced shingling/cutting of her long hair (which in her culture was a sign of cowards or mourning). She hid under a bed but was dragged out.  
> 2. **Part II: We Too Are Human Beings (Bama):**  
>    - Tamil Dalit writer remembering her 3rd-grade walk home.  
>    - Witnessed an elderly man carrying a packet of vadai by a string without touching it to hand it to the upper-caste landlord.  
>    - Annan's advice: *"Because we are born into this community, we are stripped of all honor and dignity. But if we study and make progress, we can throw away these indignities."* Education is the ultimate equalizer!

## ⚡ 8. 1-Minute Quick Recap
- Zitkala-Sa: Native American, fought against cultural erasure and cutting of hair
- Bama: Dalit girl in Tamil Nadu, experienced untouchability
- Annan's mantra: Hard work and education shatter caste barriers

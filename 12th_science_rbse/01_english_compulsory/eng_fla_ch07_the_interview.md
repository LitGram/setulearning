## 🌟 1. Overview & Strategy

> **Teacher's Direct Advice:**  
> Chapter 7 is in two distinct parts:  
> 1. **Part I: Opinions on Interviews:**  
>    - Celebrities despise interviews: V.S. Naipaul (wounds people and takes part of them), Lewis Carroll (horror of lionization), Rudyard Kipling (immoral, a crime, an assault), H.G. Wells (an ordeal, yet interviewed Stalin), Saul Bellow (thumbprints on windpipe).  
>    - Yet, Denis Brian notes that the interviewer holds an unprecedented position of power and influence.  
> 2. **Part II: Umberto Eco Interview by Mukund Padmanabhan:**  
>    - Eco considers himself a university professor who writes novels only on Sundays.  
>    - Philosophical secret: **Interstices (empty spaces)** — utilizing the small gaps of time (e.g. waiting for an elevator to ascend).  
>    - Massive success of *The Name of the Rose* (sold 10-15 million copies): A medieval mystery combining metaphysics, theology, and detective fiction.

## 🌟 1. Overview & Strategy

> **Teacher's Direct Advice:**  
> An excerpt from Keats's poetic romance *Endymion*:  
> 1. **Eternal Joy:** *"A thing of beauty is a joy forever / Its loveliness increases; it will never / Pass into nothingness."* Beauty provides a quiet bower, peaceful sleep, and sweet dreams.  
> 2. **Counteracting Human Gloom:** Despite despondence, inhuman dearth of noble natures, gloomy days, and unhealthy ways, some shape of beauty lifts the dark pall from our spirits.  
> 3. **Catalog of Beautiful Objects:** The sun, moon, old and young trees (shady boon for simple sheep), daffodils in green world, clear rills, musk-rose blooms, the grand tales of mighty dead, and the immortal fountain pouring from heaven's brink.

---

## ⚡ 8. 1-Minute Quick Recap
- Beauty never dies; its loveliness increases forever
- 'Quiet bower' = Shelter of peace and solace
- 'Dark pall' = Blanket of sorrow and despair removed by beauty
- 'Immortal drink' = Eternal inspiration from nature and art

## 🌟 1. Overview & Strategy

> **Teacher's Direct Advice:**  
> 'The Rattrap' is Selma Lagerlöf's masterpiece blending fairy-tale narrative with profound humanist philosophy:  
> 1. **The Central Metaphor:** The world is nothing but a giant rattrap setting baits of riches, food, and joy. When someone touches the bait, the trap snaps shut.  
> 2. **Three Key Encounters:**  
>    - The Crofter: Hospitable, gave him porridge and tobacco, showed him his 30 kronor; the peddler stole the money next morning (succumbing to bait).  
>    - The Ironmaster: Mistook the peddler for Captain Nils von Olof; invited him home out of patronizing pride; wanted to summon the sheriff upon discovering the truth.  
>    - Edla Willmansson: Showed genuine compassion, interceded on his behalf, treated him like an honored captain, sparking his moral transformation.  
> 3. **The Climax & Signature:** The peddler leaves behind the stolen 30 kronor, a small rattrap for Edla, and signs the note as **"Captain von Stahle"** because her respect elevated him to live up to that dignity.

## 📝 4. Solved Board Models (Step-by-Step with Marking Scheme)

### Model 1: How does the character of Edla Willmansson differ from that of her father, the ironmaster? (4 Marks)
**Answer:**  
1. **Perception & Insight:** While the father quickly mistook the ragged vagabond for his old regimental comrade in the dim forge light, Edla immediately observed his frightened eyes and judged that he was either a thief or had escaped from jail. (1 Mark)  
2. **Attitude towards Hospitality:** The father's kindness was conditional upon the stranger being a military officer; upon discovering the mistake, he angrily ordered him to leave. Edla insisted on keeping their promise of Christmas hospitality to a homeless wanderer. (1.5 Marks)  
3. **Human Dignity & Empathy:** Edla treated the peddler with unconditional respect, warmth, and trust. Her genuine compassion unlocked his latent goodness, whereas the ironmaster's haughty pride only provoked defensive resentment. (1.5 Marks)

- The world is a rattrap; 30 kronor was the cheese/bait
- Crofter's hospitality abused by peddler
- Ironmaster invited out of mistaken identity; Edla hosted out of pure Christian empathy
- The peddler signed as Captain von Stahle because her trust restored his nobility

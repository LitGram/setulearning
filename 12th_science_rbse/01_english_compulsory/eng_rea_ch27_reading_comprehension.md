## 🌟 1. Note-Making Master Rules

1. **Title:** Short, central theme of the passage.  
2. **Indentation:** Structured decimal numbering:  
   - 1. Main Heading  
     - 1.1 Sub-heading  
     - 1.2 Sub-heading  
       - 1.2.1 Sub-sub-heading  
3. **Abbreviations & Symbols (Minimum 4):** Standard abbreviations (e.g. *govt., edu., imp., info., &*) with a neat **Key to Abbreviations** box.  
4. **No Full Sentences in Notes:** Use phrases only.  
5. **Summary:** 50 words strictly condensed from the notes.

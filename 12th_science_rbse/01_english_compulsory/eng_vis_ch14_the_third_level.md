## 🌟 1. Overview & Strategy

> **Teacher's Direct Advice:**  
> Jack Finney's classic story blending science fiction with psychological escapism:  
> 1. **The Core Mystery:** Grand Central Station in New York officially has only two levels. Charley, a 31-year-old ordinary commuter, claims to have found a hidden third level.  
> 2. **Psychiatrist Sam Weiner's Verdict:** A "waking-dream wish fulfillment" — an escapist flight from the insecurities, wars, worries, and stress of modern life. Stamp collection (philately) is another temporary refuge.  
> 3. **The 1894 Atmosphere:** Brass spittoons, flickering open-flame gas lights, derby hats, handlebar mustaches, Currier & Ives locomotive, and the newspaper *The World* dated June 11, 1894.  
> 4. **The Climax:** Charley exchanges modern currency for old bills to buy two tickets to Galesburg, Illinois. Later, he discovers a first-day cover from Sam Weiner mailed from 1894 Galesburg, proving the third level is real!

## ⚡ 8. 1-Minute Quick Recap
- Protagonist: Charley (31), wife Louisa
- Setting: Grand Central Station Third Level (Galesburg, Illinois, 1894)
- Symbolism: Nostalgia for pre-WWI peaceful rural America
- Twist: Psychiatrist Sam himself escapes to 1894 Galesburg

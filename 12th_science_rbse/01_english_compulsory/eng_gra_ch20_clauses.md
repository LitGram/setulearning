## 🌟 1. Master Formula & Types of Clauses

1. **Relative / Adjective Clauses:**
   - **Who:** For persons (Subject): *The man who helped me was a doctor.*
   - **Whom:** For persons (Object): *The boy whom you met is my brother.*
   - **Whose:** For possession: *This is the girl whose bag was stolen.*
   - **Which:** For animals & inanimate things: *The book which is on the table is mine.*
   - **Where / When / Why:** For place, time, and reason.

2. **Noun Clauses:**
   - Functions as Subject, Object, or Complement: *I know that honesty is the best policy.* / *What he said is true.*

3. **Adverb Clauses:**
   - Time (*when, while, as soon as*), Condition (*if, unless*), Reason (*because, as, since*), Concession (*although, though*).

---

## 📝 4. Solved Board Models

1. Join using **'who'**: *The lady is a teacher. She lives next door.*  
   $\implies$ **The lady who lives next door is a teacher.**  
2. Join using **'whose'**: *I saw a boy. His bicycle was punctured.*  
   $\implies$ **I saw a boy whose bicycle was punctured.**  
3. Join using **'where'**: *This is the school. I studied here.*  
   $\implies$ **This is the school where I studied.**

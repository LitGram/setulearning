### 🌟 पाठ का केंद्रीय भाव एवं मुख्य विषय (Chapter Essence & Core Themes)

#### Story I: His First Flight (Liam O'Flaherty)
1. **Conquering Fear and Self-Doubt (भय और आत्म-संदेह पर विजय):**
   - The young seagull's two brothers and little sister had much shorter wings than his, yet they ran to the brink, flapped their wings, and flew away. The young seagull lacked confidence in his own wings, terrified by the sheer expanse of the sea below.
   - *हिन्दी भाव:* छोटे समुद्री बगुले के भाई-बहन जिनके पंख उससे भी छोटे थे, उड़ गए; परंतु वह अगाध समुद्र को देखकर डर गया कि उसके पंख उसका साथ नहीं देंगे।
2. **The familys response (माता-पिता की प्रतिक्रिया):**
   - The parents scolded him and threatened to let him starve on the ledge unless he flew. Later, the mother brought a piece of fish and held it just beyond his reach; hunger helped prompt him to dive.
   - She held a piece of fish almost within his reach. Maddened by hunger, he dived towards it.
   - *हिन्दी भाव:* माता-पिता ने उसे डांटा और भूखा रहने की चेतावनी दी। अंततः माँ ने मछली का टुकड़ा दिखाकर उसकी भूख को उसकी उड़ान की शक्ति बना दिया।
3. **The Ecstasy of Self-Realization (आत्म-विश्वास का परमानंद):**
   - The moment he plunged, monstrous terror seized him, but a minute later his wings spread outward, wind rushed against his breast feathers, and he discovered he was not falling headlong but soaring gracefully.

#### Story II: The Black Aeroplane (Frederick Forsyth)
1. **The Peril of Complacency and Reckless Desire (अति-उत्साह और खतरे की अनदेखी):**
   - The narrator was flying his old Dakota airplane from France to England under a serene starlit sky, dreaming of joining his family for a grand English breakfast.
   - When massive storm clouds resembling black mountains blocked his path, his longing for home made him take the reckless decision to fly straight into the tempest rather than returning to Paris.
   - *हिन्दी भाव:* पायलट अपने घर पहुँचकर परिवार के साथ 'अंग्रेजी नाश्ता' करने की लालसा में तूफानी बादलों को देखकर भी पेरिस वापस लौटने के बजाय खतरे में सीधा घुस जाता है।
2. **The Mystery of the Black Aeroplane (काले रहस्यमयी विमान का विस्मय):**
   - Inside the storm, the compass, radio and other instruments failed. Another pilot appeared in a black aeroplane with no lights on its wings and signalled him to follow.
   - After landing, the narrator asked the woman at the control centre about the other aeroplane. She said the Dakota was the only one she could see on the radar.
   - *हिन्दी भाव:* जब उसके सारे यंत्र बंद हो गए, तो एक अनाम काले विमान के पायलट ने उसे रास्ता दिखाया। सुरक्षित उतरने पर पता चला कि रडार पर उसके अलावा कोई दूसरा विमान था ही नहीं!
3. **Providence, Willpower, or Subconscious Hallucination (ईश्वरीय कृपा या अंतर्मन का विश्वास):**
   - The story leaves the black aeroplane's identity unexplained. The NCERT teacher's note explicitly invites discussion about whether it was real or imagined; supernatural aid is one possible interpretation, not a stated fact.

## 🎭 2. Deep Character Sketches & Key Figures (Traits, actions, motivations, quotes)

### 1. The Young Seagull (Protagonist of Story I / युवा समुद्री बगुला)
* **Initial State & Psychological Hurdles:**
  - Plagued by extreme lack of self-belief; convinced his wings would never support his weight.
  - Watched his parents coaching his siblings in perfecting the art of flight, skimming the waves, and diving for herring.
  - Had been alone on the ledge for about 24 hours and had not eaten since the previous nightfall.
  - *हिन्दी विवरण:* अत्यधिक भयभीत, आत्म-विश्वास विहीन और भूख से व्याकुल पक्षी, जिसे लगता था कि समुद्र में कूदते ही वह डूब जाएगा।
* **The Turning Point & Transformation:**
  - Maddened by the sight of food held by his mother, he surrendered to primal survival instinct and took the fateful leap.
  - Fear dissolved within seconds, replaced by the triumphant thrill of flapping his wings and floating on the green sea.
* **Key Textual Quotes:**
  - *"He felt certain that his wings would never support him; so he bent his head and ran away back to the little hole."*
  - *"Maddened by hunger, he dived at the fish."*
  - *"He was not falling headlong now. He was soaring gradually downwards and outwards. He was no longer afraid."*

### 2. The Mother Seagull (माता बगुला)
* **Actions in the story:**
  - She called to the young seagull, and later picked up a piece of fish and flew towards him.
  - When the fish was almost within his reach, she halted; he dived towards it.
  - A possible interpretation is that her action helps him make his first flight. The story does not state her private reasoning or set out a general parenting rule.
* *हिन्दी विवरण:* एक बुद्धिमान माँ जिसने अपने बच्चे के डर को खत्म करने के लिए भूख को हथियार बनाया और उसे उड़ने पर विवश कर दिया।

### 3. The Dakota Pilot (Narrator of Story II / डेकोटा का साहसी पायलट)
* **Personality & Emotional Drive:**
  - Flying an old Dakota DS 088 across France towards England at 1:30 AM.
  - Nostalgic, family-oriented, and overly eager for a peaceful holiday and a traditional English breakfast.
  - **Risk-taking:** Although he considers returning to Paris, he chooses to fly into the storm because he wants to get home.
* **Resilience & Gratitude:**
  - Did not panic completely when instruments failed; showed faith by following the stranger's hand gestures like an obedient child.
  - Deeply respectful and desperate to thank his savior upon landing.
* **Key Textual Quotes:**
  - *"I ought to go back to Paris, I thought, but I wanted to get home. I wanted that breakfast. 'I'll take the risk,' I thought, and flew that old Dakota straight into the storm."*
  - *"He turned his aeroplane slowly to the north, in front of my Dakota, so that it would be easier for me to follow him."*
  - *"Who was the pilot on the strange black aeroplane, flying in the storm, without lights?"*

### 4. The Mysterious Pilot in the Black Aeroplane (अनाम देवदूत / रहस्यमयी पायलट)
* **Supernatural / Psychological Manifestation:**
  - Flew an airplane with no lights on its wings right alongside the Dakota in the middle of blinding black storm clouds.
  - Waved his hand and signalled the narrator to follow; the narrator later found the sky empty.
  - The control-centre woman says the Dakota was the only aeroplane she could see on the radar; the story does not explain who or what the other pilot was.

## 📜 3. Key Extract-Based Passages with Board Model Questions & Detailed Explanations

### 📖 Extract 1: The Young Seagull's Terror & Parental Admonition (Story I)
```text
"The young seagull was alone on his ledge. His two brothers and his sister had already flown 
away the day before. He had been afraid to fly with them. Somehow when he had taken a little 
run forward to the brink of the ledge and attempted to flap his wings he became afraid. 
The great expanse of sea stretched down beneath, and it was such a long way down — miles down. 
He felt certain that his wings would never support him; so he bent his head and ran away back 
to the little hole under the ledge where he slept at night.
Even when each of his brothers and his little sister, whose wings were far shorter than his own, 
ran to the brink, flapped their wings, and flew away, he failed to muster up courage to take 
that plunge which appeared to him so desperate."
```

* **Glossary & Meanings (कठिन शब्दार्थ):**
  - **Ledge:** A narrow horizontal shelf projecting from a wall or cliff (चट्टान का उभरा हुआ किनारा).
  - **Brink:** The extreme edge of land before a steep drop (कगार / किनारा).
  - **Expanse:** A wide, continuous area of land or water (विशाल फैलाव).
  - **Muster up:** Gather / summon up with effort (हिम्मत जुटाना).
  - **Plunge:** A sudden dive or jump from a high place (छलांग लगाना).
* **Text-based practice questions and suggested answers:**
  1. **Why was the young seagull alone on the ledge?**
     - *Answer:* He was alone because his brothers and sister had already flown away the previous day, while he had been terrified to fly with them.
  2. **What convinced the young seagull not to fly?**
     - *Answer:* The vast expanse of the deep sea miles beneath him convinced him that his wings would never support his weight.
  3. **How did his siblings compare to him in terms of wing size?**
     - *Answer:* His brothers and sister had wings that were far shorter than his own, yet they flew confidently.
  4. **Find a phrase in the passage that means 'gather or collect courage'.**
     - *Answer:* Muster up courage.
  5. **Where did the young seagull sleep at night?**
     - *Answer:* In a little hole under the ledge.

### 📖 Extract 2: Maddened by Hunger — The Leap of Faith (Story I)
```text
"He waited a moment in surprise, wondering why she did not come nearer, and then, maddened 
by hunger, he dived at the fish. With a loud scream he fell outwards and downwards into space. 
Then a monstrous terror seized him and his heart stood still. He could hear nothing. But it 
only lasted a minute. The next moment he felt his wings spread outwards. The wind rushed 
against his breast feathers, then under his stomach, and against his wings. He could feel 
the tips of his wings cutting through the air. He was not falling headlong now. He was soaring 
gradually downwards and outwards. He was no longer afraid."
```

* **Glossary & Meanings (कठिन शब्दार्थ):**
  - **Maddened by hunger:** Driven crazy by extreme starvation (भूख से बावला हो जाना).
  - **Monstrous terror:** Huge, overpowering fear (अति-भयानक आतंक).
  - **Seized:** Gripped tightly (जकड़ लिया).
  - **Soaring:** Flying high in the air without flapping hard (हवा में ऊपर उठना/उड़ना).
  - **Headlong:** Falling with head first / diving uncontrollably (सिर के बल गिरना).
* **Text-based practice questions and suggested answers:**
  1. **What compelled the young seagull to finally dive?**
     - *Answer:* He was driven mad by extreme hunger when he saw the fish in his mother's beak, which made him dive blindly at the food.
  2. **What happened immediately after he fell into space?**
     - *Answer:* A monstrous terror gripped him and his heart stood still, but this paralyzed fear lasted only for about a minute.
  3. **How did he realize he was flying rather than plummeting to death?**
     - *Answer:* He felt his wings spreading outward, the wind rushing against his breast feathers, and the tips of his wings slicing through the air.
  4. **Find the word in the passage that means 'flying gracefully upward or through air'.**
     - *Answer:* Soaring.
  5. **What emotional change occurred in the young seagull at the end of the passage?**
     - *Answer:* His overpowering fear vanished completely and was replaced by the joyful thrill of flight.

### 📖 Extract 3: Flying the Old Dakota into the Black Storm (Story II)
```text
"Paris was about 150 kilometres behind me when I saw the clouds. Storm clouds. They were huge. 
They looked like black mountains standing in front of me across the sky. I knew I could not fly 
up and over them, and I did not have enough fuel to fly around them to the north or south.
'I ought to go back to Paris,' I thought, but I wanted to get home. I wanted that breakfast.
'I'll take the risk,' I thought, and flew that old Dakota straight into the storm.
Inside the clouds, everything was suddenly black. It was impossible to see anything outside 
the aeroplane. The old aeroplane jumped and twisted in the air. I looked at the compass. 
I couldn't believe my eyes: the compass was turning round and round and round. It was dead. 
It would not work. The other instruments were suddenly dead, too. I tried the radio."
```

* **Glossary & Meanings (कठिन शब्दार्थ):**
  - **Storm clouds:** Ominous, heavy rain clouds (तूफानी बादल).
  - **Huge:** Enormous / gigantic (विशालकाय).
  - **Instruments:** Measuring gauges like compass, altimeter, and speedometer (विमान के यंत्र).
  - **Dead:** Ceased to function (खराब हो गए / बंद पड़ गए).
  - **Twisted:** Turned sharply and violently (हवा में हिचकोले खाना).
* **Text-based practice questions and suggested answers:**
  1. **What did the storm clouds look like to the pilot?**
     - *Answer:* The storm clouds looked like gigantic black mountains standing across the sky.
  2. **Why didn't the pilot fly around the storm clouds?**
     - *Answer:* He did not have enough fuel left in his tanks to fly around them to the north or south.
  3. **Why did the pilot decide to take the risk instead of returning to Paris?**
     - *Answer:* He was desperate to reach home to celebrate the holiday and enjoy an English breakfast with his family.
  4. **What happened to the instruments inside the storm?**
     - *Answer:* The compass turned round and round and went dead, followed by the failure of all other instruments including the radio.
  5. **Which airplane was the narrator flying?**
     - *Answer:* The narrator was flying an old Dakota with the call-sign DS 088.

### 📖 Extract 4: The Revelation in the Control Tower (Story II)
```text
"I landed and was not sorry to walk away from the old Dakota near the control tower. 
I went and asked a woman in the control centre where I was and who the other pilot was. 
I wanted to say 'Thank you'.
She looked at me very strangely, and then laughed.
'Another aeroplane? Up there in this storm? No other aeroplanes were flying tonight. 
Yours was the only one I could see on the radar.'
So who helped me to arrive there safely without a compass or a radio, and without any more 
fuel in my tanks? Who was the pilot on the strange black aeroplane, flying in the storm, 
without lights?"
```

* **Glossary & Meanings (कठिन शब्दार्थ):**
  - **Control tower:** Building at an airport that directs air traffic (नियंत्रण कक्ष/टावर).
  - **Strangely:** In an odd or unusual manner (अजीब तरीके से).
  - **Radar:** A system using radio waves to detect airplanes and ships (रडार यंत्र).
  - **Without lights:** Unilluminated (बिना रोशनी के).
* **Text-based practice questions and suggested answers:**
  1. **Why did the narrator go to the control centre immediately after landing?**
     - *Answer:* He went to inquire about the identity of the pilot of the black aeroplane so that he could thank him for saving his life.
  2. **How did the woman in the control room react to his inquiry?**
     - *Answer:* She looked at him very strangely, laughed in disbelief, and told him that no other planes were flying in the storm.
  3. **What shocking revelation did the radar show?**
     - *Answer:* The woman said the Dakota was the only aeroplane she could see on the radar.
  4. **Find a word from the passage that means 'in an unusual or peculiar manner'.**
     - *Answer:* Strangely.
  5. **What question does the story leave unanswered at its conclusion?**
     - *Answer:* It leaves unanswered the mystery of who the pilot of the black aeroplane was that guided him safely to the runway.

## 🎯 4. 8 Comprehensive Solved Model Archetypes (Practice model answers)

### 📌 Archetype 1: Mother's Ingenuity in 'His First Flight' (practice answer; suggested length 60–80 words)
* **Question:** How did the mother seagull succeed in making the young seagull fly when all other methods had failed?
* **Step-by-Step Model Answer:**
  1. **The parents' earlier efforts:** They had called to him, scolded him and threatened to let him starve, but he still would not fly.
  2. **The mother's action:** She picked up a piece of fish and flew towards him, stopping when it was almost within reach.
  3. **The turning point:** Maddened by hunger, the young bird dived at the fish. As he fell, his wings spread and he began to fly.

---

### 📌 Archetype 2: Theme of Conquering Fear in 'His First Flight' (practice answer; suggested length 60–80 words)
* **Question:** "Fear is merely in the mind; once confronted, it vanishes." Elaborate this statement with reference to Liam O'Flaherty's story.
* **Step-by-Step Model Answer:**
  - **Fear and doubt:** The young seagull believed his wings would not support him, even though his siblings, whose wings were shorter, had flown.
  - **The first flight:** When he plunged from the ledge, he was terrified. He then felt his wings spread and the air move against them, and realised he was soaring rather than falling headlong.
  - **Possible interpretation:** His first flight can be read as a story about facing fear. Avoid presenting that reading as a scientific rule or the only possible lesson.

---

### 📌 Archetype 3: The Young Seagull's Floating on the Sea (practice answer; suggested length 40–50 words)
* **Question:** Describe the young seagull's experience when he tried to land on the green sea.
* **Step-by-Step Model Answer:**
  - After soaring and diving, he dropped his legs to stand on the green sea. When his feet sank in, he screamed with fright and attempted to flap his wings to rise again.
  - However, exhausted and weakened by hunger, he could not lift himself. But as his belly touched the water, he sank no further and floated effortlessly, surrounded by his cheering family who rewarded him with scraps of dogfish.

---

### 📌 Archetype 4: The Risk Taken by the Dakota Pilot (practice answer; suggested length 60–80 words)
* **Question:** What was the risk the pilot of the Old Dakota took? Why did he take it, and was it a prudent decision?
* **Step-by-Step Model Answer:**
  1. **The Nature of the Risk:** The narrator flew his old Dakota, identified to Paris Control as DS 088, directly into storm clouds resembling black mountains, although he had considered turning back.
  2. **The Motive:** The decision was driven entirely by emotional nostalgia—his yearning to reach home in England, spend holidays with his family, and enjoy a traditional English breakfast.
  3. **Prudence evaluation:** Readers may judge the choice as reckless because the narrator knew the storm was dangerous and chose to enter it. Inside, his compass, radio and other instruments failed; the story leaves the rescue unexplained.

---

### 📌 Archetype 5: The Appearance of the Mysterious Black Aeroplane (practice answer; suggested length 50–60 words)
* **Question:** Describe the entry and actions of the mysterious black aeroplane in the storm.
* **Step-by-Step Model Answer:**
  - When the Dakota's compass, radio and other instruments failed, the narrator saw a black aeroplane beside him. The other plane's identity is left unexplained.
  - It had no lights on its wings. The narrator saw the pilot, who raised a hand and signalled him to follow.
  - The narrator followed the aeroplane for about half an hour, until he emerged from the clouds and saw a runway. He then found the sky empty.

---

### 📌 Archetype 6: Who Was the Pilot on the Strange Black Aeroplane? (practice answer; suggested length 60–80 words)
* **Question:** "Who was the pilot on the strange black aeroplane?" Explore the two possible interpretations of this central mystery.
* **Step-by-Step Model Answer:**
  1. **Supernatural / Divine Interpretation:** The black aeroplane was a miraculous guardian angel or an act of divine providence dispatched to save a doomed pilot in response to his desperate will to live.
  2. **Possible psychological reading:** A reader might think the narrator imagined the other aeroplane under stress, but the text does not establish this or give a technical explanation.
  3. **Literary Merit:** Forsyth intentionally leaves the question unanswered to preserve the eerie, atmospheric thrill of aviation lore.

---

### 📌 Archetype 7: Contrast Between the Two Stories (practice answer; suggested length 60–80 words)
* **Question:** Compare and contrast the themes of 'His First Flight' and 'The Black Aeroplane'.
* **Step-by-Step Model Answer:**
  - **Similarity:** Both stories involve fear and flight, but one is about a young seagull's first flight and the other about a pilot caught in a storm.
  - **Contrast in Nature:**
    - *His First Flight* can be read as a story about fear, hunger and learning to fly.
    - *The Black Aeroplane* leaves the identity of the pilot who guides the narrator unresolved.

---

### 📌 Archetype 8: Role of Family in 'His First Flight' (practice answer; suggested length 40–50 words)
* **Question:** How does the young seagull's family demonstrate the ideal balance between discipline and celebration?
* **Step-by-Step Model Answer:**
  - **Discipline:** The family called and scolded him, and threatened to let him starve unless he flew.
  - **Celebration:** After he flew, his family flew around him, called out, praised him and offered him scraps of dogfish.

## ⚠️ 5. Mistake Busters: Where RBSE Students Lose Marks (सामान्य त्रुटियाँ एवं समाधान)

| ❌ Common Mistake (छात्रों की सामान्य गलती) | ⚠️ Possible issue (no fixed deduction established) | ✅ Correct Board-Standard Answer (सही उत्तर) |
|---|---|---|
| Attributing 'The Black Aeroplane' to Liam O'Flaherty. | This confuses the authors; no fixed deduction is established. | *His First Flight* is by **Liam O'Flaherty**; *The Black Aeroplane* is by **Frederick Forsyth**. |
| Writing that the young seagull's father pushed him off the ledge. | Factual error. This differs from the story. | The **mother** flew close with a fish; maddened by hunger, the chick **dived on his own**. |
| Stating that the black aeroplane had lights on its wings. | Contradicts the text's description. | The narrator says it had **no lights on its wings**. |
| Claiming to know exactly who or what the black plane was. | The text leaves the identity unexplained. | Present any explanation as an interpretation, not a confirmed fact. |
| Forgetting the call-sign of the Dakota. | Evaluators look for the exact identifier. | The aircraft was the **Old Dakota DS 088**. |
| Writing that the narrator was flying from England to France. | Inverts the direction of flight. | He was flying **from France (Paris) to England**. |
| Missing the fish species. | Textual vocabulary matters for high scores. | The fish mentioned are **herring** and **dogfish**. |

## Practice question bank (not authenticated RBSE past papers)

### 1. [Practice set] Why was the young seagull afraid to fly?
* **Model Answer:** The young seagull was afraid to fly because he lacked confidence in his wings. Looking down at the vast, endless expanse of the sea miles below him, he felt terrified that his wings would never support his weight.

### 2. [Practice set] Describe the narrator's experience as he flew the Dakota into the storm.
* **Model Answer:** Inside the storm clouds, everything turned pitch black. The old Dakota jumped and twisted violently in the air. The compass began spinning round and round uselessly, the radio and other instruments died, and the narrator was completely lost without navigation in the tempest.

### 3. [Practice set] What did the young seagull's mother do to compel him to fly?
* **Model Answer:** The mother picked up a piece of fish and flew across near the ledge. Instead of feeding him, she halted just out of reach. Maddened by extreme hunger, the young seagull dived at the fish, falling into space and naturally opening his wings to fly.

### 4. [Practice set] Why did the woman in the control room look at the pilot strangely?
* **Model Answer:** She looked at him strangely because he asked about another aeroplane. She said the Dakota was the only one she could see on the radar.

### 5. [Practice set] How did the young seagull's family celebrate his first flight?
* **Model Answer:** His family flew around him, shrieking, praising him, and diving gracefully. When he floated safely on the green sea, they offered him scraps of dogfish from their beaks to celebrate his accomplishment.

### 6. [Practice set] Why did the pilot take the risk of flying into the storm?
* **Model Answer:** The pilot took the risk because he was eager to reach his home in England to spend the holidays with his family and enjoy a hearty English breakfast.

### 7. [Practice set] What happened when the young seagull tried to stand on the green sea?
* **Model Answer:** When he dropped his legs to stand, his feet sank into the green water. He screamed in terror and tried to flap his wings, but exhausted by hunger, he sank until his belly touched the water, where he floated naturally without drowning.

### 8. [Practice set] What did the pilot of the black aeroplane do to help the narrator?
* **Model Answer:** The pilot of the black aeroplane waved his hand, signaling the narrator to follow him. He turned his plane north ahead of the Dakota and guided him safely through the dark clouds until the runway appeared.

### 9. [Practice set] How did the young seagull's parents treat him before he learned to fly?
* **Model Answer:** His parents scolded him, called to him shrilly, and threatened to let him starve on his ledge unless he flew away like his brothers and sister.

### Section C: Short-answer practice questions (suggested concise responses)
17. **Why did the young seagull pretend to be falling asleep at the brink of the ledge?**
    - *Answer:* He stood on the ledge, closed his eyes and pretended to fall asleep while his family did not pay attention to him. The story does not state his exact reason for doing so.
18. **What sights did the young seagull witness while starving on the ledge?**
    - *Answer:* He saw his parents teaching his siblings how to skim the waves and dive for fish, while his older brother caught his first herring and devoured it proudly.
19. **Why did the Dakota pilot call the compass and radio 'dead'?**
    - *Answer:* The story reports that the compass, radio and other instruments stopped working inside the storm; it does not explain the technical cause.
20. **Why was the narrator not sorry to walk away from the old Dakota after landing?**
    - *Answer:* The flight had been a terrifying brush with death; he was overwhelmingly relieved to be safely on the ground and eager to discover the identity of his savior.
21. **What lesson does 'His First Flight' convey regarding parenting?**
    - *Answer:* The episode may be read as help for this bird; it should not be treated as a general parenting rule.

### Section D: Long Answer / Value-Based Questions (practice answer; suggested length 80–100 Words)
22. **Compare how fear paralyzes action and how faith/necessity inspires triumph in both 'His First Flight' and 'The Black Aeroplane'.**
    - *Model Answer:* In *His First Flight*, a young seagull is afraid to leave the ledge. After his mother holds a piece of fish near him, hunger leads him to dive; he discovers that he can fly. In *The Black Aeroplane*, a pilot enters storm clouds, loses the use of his instruments and follows an unexplained aeroplane to a runway. One story shows a first flight; the other leaves a mystery about the pilot who appears in the storm.

23. **Do you believe the Black Aeroplane was an actual aircraft or a hallucination born of the pilot's subconscious mind? Justify your interpretation with logical textual arguments.**
    - *Model Answer:* The story does not identify the pilot of the black aeroplane or explain the radar result. The control-centre woman says the Dakota was the only aeroplane she could see on the radar, while the narrator describes following another plane through the storm. Readers may interpret the other plane as supernatural or imagine that the narrator saw it under extreme stress, but the text does not confirm either explanation. The NCERT teacher's note explicitly presents the question of whether the plane was real or imagined for discussion. A sound answer should distinguish details stated in the story from the reader's interpretation.

## ⏱️ 8. 1-Minute Rapid Revision Cheat Sheet + Answer Key + 10-Mark Self Assessment Test

### 📋 High-Yield Pre-Exam Cheat Sheet
* **Story I: His First Flight:**
  - **Author:** Liam O'Flaherty | **Protagonist:** Young Seagull on sea ledge.
  - **The Conflict:** Lacked confidence in wings; terrified of vast sea; starved for 24 hours.
  - **The Solution:** Mother held scrap of dogfish near ledge; maddened by hunger, chick dived.
  - **The Climax:** Wings spread outward; soared, landed, and floated safely on green sea.
* **Story II: The Black Aeroplane:**
  - **Author:** Frederick Forsyth | **Protagonist:** Pilot of Old Dakota DS 088.
  - **Route:** Paris (France) to England | **Motive:** English breakfast with family.
  - **The Peril:** Flew into storm clouds; compass, radio and other instruments failed, and fuel was running low.
  - **The Mystery:** Black plane with no wing lights guided him to safety; the woman said the Dakota was the only plane she could see on radar.
* **Key Vocabulary for Evaluators:** *Ledge, brink, muster up courage, monstrous terror, soaring, dogfish, Old Dakota DS 088, black mountains, compass dead, radar control room.*

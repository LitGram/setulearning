## 3. मास्टर फॉर्मेट लेआउट्स (Universal Standard Formats - Modern Block Style)

> ⚠️ **महत्वपूर्ण नियम (Golden Rule):** माध्यमिक शिक्षा बोर्ड राजस्थान (RBSE) अब आधुनिक **Full Block Format** का अनुसरण करता है। इसमें **हर पंक्ति बाईं ओर (Left Margin)** से शुरू होती है। किसी भी पंक्ति के अंत में कॉमा (,) लगाने की बाध्यता नहीं है।

---

### 📐 लेआउट 1: फॉर्मल पत्र का आधिकारिक प्रारूप (Formal Letter Format)

```
[Sender's Address / प्रेषक का पता]
12, Shanti Nagar
Raisinghnagar, Sri Ganganagar

[Date / दिनांक - Expand format like 15 March 2026]
15 March 2026

[Receiver's Designation & Full Address / प्रापक का पद एवं पता]
The Municipal Commissioner
Municipal Board
Raisinghnagar

[Subject Line / विषय - संक्षिप्त, स्पष्ट, 4-7 शब्द]
Subject: Complaint regarding insanitary condition in our locality

[Salutation / संबोधन]
Sir / Madam

[Body of the Letter - 3 स्पष्ट पैराग्राफ्स / 3-Paragraph Architecture]
Para 1: Introduction & Problem:
Through this letter, I would like to draw your kind attention towards...

Para 2: Impact / Consequences / Complications:
The sweepers do not clean the streets regularly. Heaps of garbage are lying everywhere. Foul smell is spreading and drains are overflowing. It has become a breeding ground for mosquitoes, risking a dengue outbreak.

Para 3: Action Request / Closing:
I, therefore, request you to kindly look into the matter urgently and arrange for daily sanitization. A prompt action will be highly appreciated.

[Subscription / शिष्टाचार पूर्ण समापन]
Yours faithfully / Yours sincerely

[Signature & Full Name / हस्ताक्षर एवं नाम]
Mukesh Kumar
(A concerned resident)
```

## 4. मिस्टेक बस्टर्स: बोर्ड परीक्षक कहाँ 1 से 2 अंक काटते हैं? (Mistake Busters)

| सामान्य गलती | इससे क्या समस्या हो सकती है? | बेहतर अभ्यास |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **गलती 1: 'Your's faithfully' में Apostrophe लगाना:** छात्र `Your's` या `your's` लिख देते हैं। | यह मानक लिखित अंग्रेज़ी में गलत रूप है; कोई निश्चित अंक-कटौती यहाँ प्रमाणित नहीं है। | **`Yours faithfully`** या **`Yours sincerely`** लिखें। |
| **गलती 2: संकुचित दिनांक लिखना (e.g., 12/03/26):** अंकगणितीय रूप में दिनांक लिख देना। | गैर-पेशेवर माना जाता है। | हमेशा विस्तारित दिनांक लिखें: **12 March 2026** या **March 12, 2026**। |
| **गलती 3: ई-मेल में प्राप्तकर्ता/विषय अस्पष्ट छोड़ना:** `To:` field में नाम और address गड्डमड्ड करना। | ई-मेल पूरक अभ्यास है; यह guide कोई RBSE ई-मेल format या अंक-कटौती प्रमाणित नहीं करती। | ई-मेल अभ्यास में प्राप्तकर्ता और subject अलग-अलग, स्पष्ट fields में दें। |
| **गलती 4: अनौपचारिक पत्र में Subject लिखना:** मित्र या माता-पिता को लिखते समय `Subject: Congratulating on birthday` लिख देना। | अनौपचारिक पत्र में विषय नहीं होता; यह अनावश्यक औपचारिकता है। | Informal Letter में कभी भी **Subject पंक्ति न लिखें**। सीधे Salutation से शुरू करें। |
| **गलती 5: पत्र के मध्य में दाएँ-बाएँ कूदना:** पता बाएँ, दिनांक दाएँ, समापन दाएँ लिख देना। | पुराने इंडेंटेड स्टाइल में अंक काटे जा सकते हैं। | सम्पूर्ण पत्र **Left Margin (बाईं ओर)** से ही प्रारंभ करें (Modern Block Style)। |

### 📝 मॉडल 1 (Formal - Official Complaint): ध्वनि प्रदूषण की रोकथाम हेतु उपखंड अधिकारी (SDM) को पत्र
* **प्रश्न:** You are Manish/Manisha living at 15, Civil Lines, Bharatpur. Write a formal letter to the Sub-Divisional Magistrate (SDM) of your area complaining against the nuisance of loudspeakers during examination days. (Word limit: ~80-100 words | 5 Marks)

```
15, Civil Lines
Bharatpur

20 February 2026

The Sub-Divisional Magistrate
Bharatpur Sub-Division
Bharatpur

Subject: Nuisance caused by unrestricted use of loudspeakers during examinations

Sir,

I wish to bring to your kind notice the severe harassment and mental distress caused to students by the rampant and deafening use of loudspeakers in our town.

The Board Secondary Examinations are round the corner, commencing next week. Students are preparing round the clock for their crucial tests. However, various marriage gardens, religious processions, and commercial advertisers blare loudspeakers at full volume till late midnight. The jarring noise shatters our concentration and prevents students from sleeping peacefully. Repeated personal appeals to the organizers have yielded no response.

Therefore, I earnestly request you to impose an immediate ban on the use of loudspeakers after 10:00 PM and take stringent action against defaulters.

Thanking you.

Yours faithfully,
Manish
(President, Bharatpur Students' Welfare Union)
```

### 📝 मॉडल 2 (Formal - School Authority): प्रधानाचार्य को अतिरिक्त अंग्रेजी कक्षाओं की व्यवस्था हेतु प्रार्थना पत्र

Thanking you.

Yours obediently,
Vikas

### 📝 मॉडल 3 (Formal - Letter to Editor): समाचार पत्र के संपादक को सड़क सुरक्षा एवं आवारा पशुओं की समस्या पर पत्र
* **प्रश्न:** You are Gaurav/Garima living in Jodhpur. Write a letter to the Editor of 'The Rajasthan Patrika' highlighting the menace of stray cattle on city roads and frequent road accidents. (5 Marks)

```
42, Paota 'B' Road
Jodhpur

18 April 2026

The Editor
The Rajasthan Patrika
Jodhpur

Subject: Menace of stray cattle causing fatal road accidents in Jodhpur

Sir,

Through the columns of your widely circulated and esteemed daily, I wish to draw the urgent attention of the municipal administration and traffic police toward the escalating menace of stray cattle roaming freely across city thoroughfares.

Herds of stray cows and bulls sit persistently right in the middle of busy intersections such as Sojati Gate and Paota Circle. During nocturnal hours, poor street lighting makes these animals virtually invisible to motorists, resulting in severe collisions, fatalities, and regular traffic jams. In addition, aggressive bulls fight among themselves, injuring pedestrian shoppers. The municipal cattle-catching squads seem completely inactive.

The municipal authorities must launch an aggressive drive to impound these stray animals and shift them into Gaushalas. Heavy penalties must be slapped on irresponsible cattle owners who abandon their livestock on roads.

Yours truly,
Gaurav Sharma
```

### 📝 मॉडल 4 (Formal - Official Complaint): सहायक अभियंता (AEN) को बार-बार बिजली कटौती के संबंध में पत्र
* **प्रश्न:** You are Suresh/Sunita from Ward No. 5, Sikar. Write an official letter of complaint to the Assistant Engineer (AEN), JVVNL, complaining against frequent unscheduled power cuts in your locality. (5 Marks)

```
Ward No. 5, Subhash Chowk
Sikar

12 May 2026

The Assistant Engineer (AEN)
Jaipur Vidyut Vitran Nigam Limited (JVVNL)
Sikar

Subject: Frequent unscheduled power cuts and voltage fluctuations in Ward No. 5

Sir,

I write this to express the deep distress and agony of the residents of Ward No. 5 due to persistent and unannounced power outages over the past fortnight.

Electricity is disconnected four to five times every day without prior notice, often stretching for five continuous hours during blistering summer afternoons. The severe voltage fluctuations have burned domestic electric motors, water pumps, and refrigerators. Moreover, drinking water supply gets completely paralyzed without electrical pressure. Elderly citizens and infants are suffering from heat exhaustion.

In spite of several telephone complaints to the local sub-station complaint office, no lineman has attended to the malfunctioning transformer. I request you to investigate this matter urgently, overhaul the overloaded transformer, and ensure uninterrupted power supply.

Thanking you.

Yours faithfully,
Suresh Meena
```

## 7. फटाफट परीक्षा पूर्व चीट शीट (Pre-Exam Rapid Cheat Sheet)

1. **लेआउट संरेखण:** सभी चीजें बाएँ मार्जिन (Left Margin) से शुरू करें।
2. **दिनांक शैली:** हमेशा `15 March 2026` लिखें (संख्या/माह/वर्ष)।
3. **विषय की स्पष्टता:** केवल Formal पत्र और Email में Subject लिखें (अधिकतम 5-7 शब्द)।
4. **इन्फॉर्मल में नो-सब्जेक्ट:** माता-पिता और मित्रों के पत्रों में **कभी भी Subject न लिखें**।
5. **संबोधन का चयन:**
   - अधिकारी/संपादक: `Sir / Madam`
   - मित्र: `Dear [Name]`
   - माता-पिता: `Respected Father / Dear Mother`
6. **समापन (Subscription):**
   - अधिकारियों को: `Yours faithfully`
   - शिक्षकों को: `Yours obediently`
   - संपादकों/व्यावसायिक: `Yours truly / Yours sincerely`
   - मित्रों/रिश्तेदारों को: `Yours lovingly / Your loving friend`
7. **ईमेल हेडर:** `To:` बॉक्स में अनिवार्य रूप से लोअरकेस ईमेल आईडी लिखें।
8. **शब्द सीमा:** 80-100 शब्दों में बात को स्पष्ट और मर्यादित रखें।

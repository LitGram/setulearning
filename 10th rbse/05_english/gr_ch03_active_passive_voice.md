## 🧭 1. Voice: supplementary grammar practice

Active/Passive Voice उपयोगी अतिरिक्त grammar practice है, पर इसे current RBSE board requirement या 2-mark unit न समझें। RBSE 2026–27 English grammar में कुल 8 marks हैं; इस विषय के लिए कोई अलग mark allocation सूचीबद्ध नहीं है।

### 📌 परीक्षा का स्वरूप:
- प्रश्नपत्र में वाक्य Active Voice में दिया जाता है, और छात्र को उसे Passive Voice में बदलना होता है। प्रायः Passive Voice का प्रारम्भिक कर्ता (Object shifted to Subject) प्रश्नपत्र में पहले से मुद्रित होता है, जिससे छात्र को वाक्य को सही व्याकरणिक संरचना में पूरा करना होता है।
- **उदा. बोर्ड प्रारूप:**
  * Active: *The police caught the thief.*
  * Passive: *The thief ___________________________ .*

### 💡 Active बनाम Passive का मूल अंतर:
- **Active Voice (कर्तृवाच्य):** जब वाक्य का Subject (कर्ता) स्वयं कार्य करता है और कर्ता को प्रमुखता दी जाती है।
  * $\text{Structure: } \mathbf{\text{Subject} + \text{Verb} + \text{Object}}$
  * *Example:* Ram killed Ravana. (राम ने रावण को मारा)
- **Passive Voice (कर्मवाच्य):** जब वाक्य के Object (कर्म) को प्रमुखता दी जाती है और कर्ता द्वारा किए गए कार्य का प्रभाव कर्म पर दिखाया जाता है।
  * $\text{Structure: } \mathbf{\text{Object (as Subject)} + \text{Helping Verb (Be-form)} + V_3 + \text{by} + \text{Subject (as Object)}}$
  * *Example:* Ravana was killed by Ram. (रावण राम के द्वारा मारा गया)

## ⚡ 2. The 5 Universal Golden Rules of Voice Transformation

किसी भी Active वाक्य को Passive Voice में बदलते समय निम्नलिखित **5 अपरिवर्तनीय चरणों (Five Invariable Steps)** का पालन किया जाता है:

```
Step 1: Active के Object (कर्म) को Passive का Subject (कर्ता) बनाएं।
                          ▼
Step 2: Tense के अनुसार उपयुक्त सहायक क्रिया (Helping Verb / Form of 'BE') लगाएं।
                          ▼
Step 3: Main Verb का सदैव तृतीय रूप (Past Participle - V3) ही प्रयोग करें।
                          ▼
Step 4: नए Object से पहले उपयुक्त Preposition (प्रायः 'by', विशेष मामलों में to, at, with, in) लगाएं।
                          ▼
Step 5: Active के Subject को Object (कर्म कारक) बनाकर वाक्य के अंत में रखें।
```

### 🔄 Pronoun Case Shift Table (सर्वनाम रूपान्तरण सारणी)
जब Subject और Object के स्थान बदलते हैं, तो सर्वनामों के रूप (Cases) इस प्रकार परिवर्तित होते हैं:

| Subjective Case (Nominative) | Objective Case (Accusative) |
|:---:|:---:|
| **I** | **me** |
| **We** | **us** |
| **You** | **you** |
| **He** | **him** |
| **She** | **her** |
| **It** | **it** |
| **They** | **them** |
| **Who** | **Whom** |

## 📊 3. Master Tense Conversion Matrix (काल-वार रूपान्तरण सारणी)

> ⚠️ **महत्वपूर्ण तथ्य:** अंग्रेजी में कुल 12 Tenses में से केवल **8 Tenses** का ही Passive Voice बनता है। शेष 4 Tenses (*Present Perfect Continuous, Past Perfect Continuous, Future Continuous, Future Perfect Continuous*) का मानक व्याकरण में Passive Voice नहीं बनाया जाता क्योंकि इनमें 'be' क्रिया का दोहराव (redundancy) हो जाता है।

| क्र. सं. | काल (Tense) | Active Voice Structure | Passive Voice Helping Verb + $V_3$ | आदर्श उदाहरण (Active $\rightarrow$ Passive) |
|:---:|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Present Simple** | $V_1 / (V_1 + s/es)$ <br> `do / does + not` | **is / am / are + $V_3$** | A: He writes a letter. <br> P: **A letter is written by him.** |
| **2** | **Present Continuous** | `is / am / are + V1+ing` | **is / am / are + being + $V_3$** | A: She is cooking food. <br> P: **Food is being cooked by her.** |
| **3** | **Present Perfect** | `has / have + V3` | **has been / have been + $V_3$** | A: I have solved the puzzle. <br> P: **The puzzle has been solved by me.** |
| **4** | **Past Simple** | $V_2$ <br> `did + not + V1` | **was / were + $V_3$** | A: Mohan broke the window. <br> P: **The window was broken by Mohan.** |
| **5** | **Past Continuous** | `was / were + V1+ing` | **was / were + being + $V_3$** | A: They were playing football. <br> P: **Football was being played by them.** |
| **6** | **Past Perfect** | `had + V3` | **had been + $V_3$** | A: She had finished the work. <br> P: **The work had been finished by her.** |
| **7** | **Future Simple** | `will / shall + V1` | **will be / shall be + $V_3$** | A: We shall help him. <br> P: **He will be helped by us.** |
| **8** | **Future Perfect** | `will / shall have + V3`| **will / shall have been + $V_3$** | A: They will have built the bridge. <br> P: **The bridge will have been built by them.** |

## 🔍 4. Specialized Board Transformation Patterns (विशिष्ट परीक्षा संरचनाएं)

### 📘 Pattern 4.1: Modal Auxiliaries (मॉडल्स वाले वाक्य)
- **Modals:** *can, could, may, might, must, should, would, ought to, need not, dare not, used to*.
- **Present / Future Modal Formula:**
  $$\mathbf{\text{Active: } \text{Subject} + \text{Modal} + V_1 + \text{Object}}$$
  $$\mathbf{\text{Passive: } \text{Object} + \text{Modal} + \mathbf{be} + V_3 + \text{by} + \text{Subject}}$$
  * *Example 1:* You can lift this heavy box.  
    $\rightarrow$ **This heavy box can be lifted by you.**
  * *Example 2:* We should respect our elders.  
    $\rightarrow$ **Our elders should be respected by us.**
  * *Example 3:* One must keep one's promises.  
    $\rightarrow$ **Promises must be kept.** (यहाँ 'one' लोप हो गया)
- **Past Modal Formula (Modal + have + $V_3$):**
  $$\mathbf{\text{Active: } \text{Subject} + \text{Modal} + \text{have} + V_3 + \text{Object}}$$
  $$\mathbf{\text{Passive: } \text{Object} + \text{Modal} + \text{have been} + V_3 + \text{by} + \text{Subject}}$$
  * *Example:* You should have helped the poor boy.  
    $\rightarrow$ **The poor boy should have been helped by you.**

### 📗 Pattern 4.2: Sentences with Two Objects (द्वि-कर्मक वाक्य — Direct & Indirect Object)
जब Active वाक्य में दो Objects दिए हों:
1. **Indirect Object (अप्रत्यक्ष कर्म):** सजीव व्यक्ति (Person) $\rightarrow$ *me, him, her, us, them, you, Ramesh*.
2. **Direct Object (प्रत्यक्ष कर्म):** निर्जीव वस्तु (Thing) $\rightarrow$ *a book, a pen, money, a letter*.

*Active Example:* **Mr. Sharma teaches us English.**
- यहाँ *us* = Indirect Object (Person), *English* = Direct Object (Thing).
- ऐसे वाक्यों को Passive में बदलने की **दो विधियां** हैं:

#### 🌟 Method 1 (Indirect Object को कर्ता बनाकर — Recommended & Preferred in RBSE Board):
$$\mathbf{\text{Indirect Object} + \text{Helping Verb} + V_3 + \text{Direct Object} + \text{by} + \text{Subject}}$$
* *Passive 1:* **We are taught English by Mr. Sharma.** (सबसे सरल और सर्वाधिक प्रामाणिक)

#### 🌟 Method 2 (Direct Object को कर्ता बनाकर):
जब निर्जीव वस्तु को कर्ता बनाया जाता है, तो सजीव व्यक्ति से पूर्व **'to'** (या कभी-कभी 'for') लगाना अनिवार्य होता है:
$$\mathbf{\text{Direct Object} + \text{Helping Verb} + V_3 + \mathbf{to} + \text{Indirect Object} + \text{by} + \text{Subject}}$$
* *Passive 2:* **English is taught to us by Mr. Sharma.**

#### अधिक उदाहरण:
- *Active:* My father gave me a watch.
  * *Passive A:* **I was given a watch by my father.** (Method 1)
  * *Passive B:* **A watch was given to me by my father.** (Method 2)
- *Active:* He told us an interesting story.
  * *Passive A:* **We were told an interesting story by him.**
  * *Passive B:* **An interesting story was told to us by him.**

### 📙 Pattern 4.3: Imperative Sentences (आज्ञा, प्रार्थना एवं परामर्श वाले वाक्य)
आज्ञावाचक वाक्यों में Subject (you) छिपा रहता है और वाक्य सीधे $V_1$ से प्रारम्भ होता है।

#### 1. Orders / Commands (आज्ञा या निर्देश):
- **Formula:** $\mathbf{\text{Let} + \text{Object} + \mathbf{be} + V_3}$
  * *Active:* Shut the door. $\rightarrow$ **Let the door be shut.**
  * *Active:* Open the window. $\rightarrow$ **Let the window be opened.**
  * *Active:* Post this letter. $\rightarrow$ **Let this letter be posted.**
  * *Alternative Structure:* **You are ordered / commanded to + $V_1$ + Object.**
  * *Active:* Stand up on the bench. $\rightarrow$ **You are ordered to stand up on the bench.**

#### 2. Requests (प्रार्थना — Please / Kindly वाले वाक्य):
'Please' या 'Kindly' को पूरी तरह हटा दें और वाक्य के प्रारम्भ में **"You are requested to"** जोड़ दें:
- **Formula:** $\mathbf{\text{You are requested to} + V_1 + \text{Rest of Sentence}}$
  * *Active:* Please help me. $\rightarrow$ **You are requested to help me.**
  * *Active:* Kindly grant me leave for two days. $\rightarrow$ **You are requested to grant me leave for two days.**
  * *Active:* Please do not smoke here. $\rightarrow$ **You are requested not to smoke here.**

#### 3. Moral Advice / Suggestions (सलाह / परामर्श / नैतिक कर्तव्य):
- **Formula:** $\mathbf{\text{Object} + \mathbf{should be} + V_3}$
  * *Active:* Help the poor. $\rightarrow$ **The poor should be helped.**
  * *Active:* Respect your teachers. $\rightarrow$ **Your teachers should be respected.**
  * *Active:* Obey traffic rules. $\rightarrow$ **Traffic rules should be obeyed.**
  * *Alternative:* **You are advised to + $V_1$ + Object.**
  * *Active:* Work hard. $\rightarrow$ **You are advised to work hard.**

#### 4. Negative Imperative Sentences (Don't वाले वाक्य):
- **Formula A:** $\mathbf{\text{Let} + \text{Object} + \mathbf{not be} + V_3}$
  * *Active:* Don't pluck flowers. $\rightarrow$ **Let flowers not be plucked.**
- **Formula B:** $\mathbf{\text{You are forbidden to} + V_1 + \text{Object}}$ (सावधान: 'forbidden' के साथ 'not' नहीं लगता!)
  * *Active:* Don't touch the live wire. $\rightarrow$ **You are forbidden to touch the live wire.**

### 📕 Pattern 4.4: Omission of Unimportant Agents (अनावश्यक कर्ता का लोप)
जब Active Voice का Subject कोई **अस्पष्ट, अज्ञात, सर्वविदित या महत्वहीन** शब्द हो, तो Passive Voice में **'by + Subject'** को नहीं लिखा जाता:

$$\mathbf{\text{Unimportant Agents: someone, somebody, anyone, anybody, people, they, we, one, all, nobody, no one}}$$

- *Example 1:* Someone has stolen my purse.  
  $\rightarrow$ **My purse has been stolen.** (by someone नहीं लिखा जाता)
- *Example 2:* People speak English all over the world.  
  $\rightarrow$ **English is spoken all over the world.**
- *Example 3:* The police arrested the burglar.  
  $\rightarrow$ **The burglar was arrested.** (चोर को गिरफ्तार केवल पुलिस ही करती है, अतः *by the police* अनावश्यक है)
- *Example 4:* Doctors treat patients.  
  $\rightarrow$ **Patients are treated.**

#### ⚠️ 'Nobody / No one' का विशेष नियम (Negative Transfer):
चूंकि 'Nobody' और 'No one' नकारात्मक होते हैं, इसलिए जब इन्हें हटाया जाता है, तो Passive Voice में **'not'** जोड़ना अनिवार्य होता है:
- *Active:* Nobody answered my question.  
  $\rightarrow$ **My question was not answered.** (❌ was answered by nobody गलत है!)
- *Active:* No one can climb this steep hill.  
  $\rightarrow$ **This steep hill cannot be climbed.**

### 📓 Pattern 4.5: Verbs Taking Prepositions Other Than 'BY' (विशिष्ट Preposition नियम)
कुछ विशिष्ट क्रियाओं के बाद Passive Voice में 'by' के स्थान पर निश्चित Prepositions (*to, at, with, in*) का प्रयोग होता है। RBSE बोर्ड इन पर प्रश्न पूछकर छात्रों को परखता है:

| क्रिया (Verb) | आवश्यक Preposition | आदर्श उदाहरण (Active $\rightarrow$ Passive) |
|---|:---:|---|
| **know, marry** | **to** | A: I know him. $\rightarrow$ P: **He is known to me.** (by me नहीं!) <br> A: Ram married Sita. $\rightarrow$ P: **Sita was married to Ram.** |
| **surprise, shock, astonish, amaze, alarm, distress** | **at** | A: His sudden failure surprised me. $\rightarrow$ P: **I was surprised at his sudden failure.** <br> A: The news shocked us. $\rightarrow$ P: **We were shocked at the news.** |
| **please, satisfy, disgust, line, fill** | **with** | A: His performance pleased the teacher. $\rightarrow$ P: **The teacher was pleased with his performance.** <br> A: Smoke filled the room. $\rightarrow$ P: **The room was filled with smoke.** |
| **contain, interest, embody** | **in** | A: This jug contains milk. $\rightarrow$ P: **Milk is contained in this jug.** <br> A: This book interests me. $\rightarrow$ P: **I am interested in this book.** |

### 📒 Pattern 4.6: Interrogative Sentences (प्रश्नवाचक वाक्य)
प्रश्नवाचक वाक्य का Passive रूप भी सदैव **प्रश्नवाचक ही रहता है** (प्रारम्भ में Helping Verb तथा अंत में '?' बना रहेगा)।

#### 1. Yes/No Type (Helping Verb से प्रारम्भ होने वाले):
- Active: $\text{Do / Does} + \text{Sub} + V_1 + \text{Obj}?$  
  $\rightarrow$ Passive: $\mathbf{\text{Is / Am / Are} + \text{Obj} + V_3 + \text{by} + \text{Sub}?}$
  * *Active:* Do you play cricket? $\rightarrow$ **Is cricket played by you?**
  * *Active:* Does she write letters? $\rightarrow$ **Are letters written by her?**
- Active: $\text{Did} + \text{Sub} + V_1 + \text{Obj}?$  
  $\rightarrow$ Passive: $\mathbf{\text{Was / Were} + \text{Obj} + V_3 + \text{by} + \text{Sub}?}$
  * *Active:* Did you break this glass? $\rightarrow$ **Was this glass broken by you?**
- Active: $\text{Has / Have / Had} + \text{Sub} + V_3 + \text{Obj}?$  
  $\rightarrow$ Passive: $\mathbf{\text{Has / Have / Had} + \text{Obj} + \mathbf{been} + V_3 + \text{by} + \text{Sub}?}$
  * *Active:* Have you completed the project? $\rightarrow$ **Has the project been completed by you?**

#### 2. Wh- Type Questions:
Wh- शब्द (जैसे *Why, When, Where, How, What*) अपने स्थान पर यथावत रहेगा:
$$\mathbf{\text{Wh-word} + \text{Helping Verb} + \text{Object} + V_3 + \text{by} + \text{Subject}?}$$
- *Active:* Why did you punish him? $\rightarrow$ **Why was he punished by you?**
- *Active:* When will you return my camera? $\rightarrow$ **When will my camera be returned by you?**

#### 3. 'Who' एवं 'Whom' वाले प्रश्न (Super-Important Rule!):
- **Who $\rightarrow$ By whom + Helping Verb + Object + $V_3$?**
  * *Active:* Who wrote the Ramayana? $\rightarrow$ **By whom was the Ramayana written?**
  * *Active:* Who has broken this mirror? $\rightarrow$ **By whom has this mirror been broken?**
- **Whom $\rightarrow$ Who + Helping Verb + $V_3$ + by + Subject?**
  * *Active:* Whom did you invite? $\rightarrow$ **Who was invited by you?**

### 📘 Pattern 4.7: Infinitive Structures ('to + $V_1$') & Going to
- **Formula:** $\mathbf{\text{to} + V_1 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \text{to} + \mathbf{be} + V_3}$
  * *Active:* I have to do this work. $\rightarrow$ **This work has to be done by me.**
  * *Active:* She has to write two essays. $\rightarrow$ **Two essays have to be written by her.**
  * *Active:* It is time to close the shop. $\rightarrow$ **It is time for the shop to be closed.**
  * *Active:* There is no money to spend. $\rightarrow$ **There is no money to be spent.**
- **'Going to' Construction:**
  $$\mathbf{\text{Subject} + \text{be} + \text{going to} + V_1 + \text{Object} \quad \Longrightarrow \quad \text{Object} + \text{be} + \text{going to} + \mathbf{be} + V_3 + \text{by} + \text{Sub}}$$
  * *Active:* He is going to buy a new bicycle. $\rightarrow$ **A new bicycle is going to be bought by him.**
  * *Active:* They are going to build a hospital. $\rightarrow$ **A hospital is going to be built by them.**
  * *Active:* The teacher is going to ask questions. $\rightarrow$ **Questions are going to be asked by the teacher.**

---

### 📙 Pattern 4.8: Complex Sentences & "People say that..." Clauses (जटिल उपवाक्यों का वाच्य)
जब Active वाक्य का ढांचा **"People say that...", "They believe that...", "We know that...", "Everyone considers that..."** जैसा हो, तो इसे Passive में बदलने की दो विधियां हैं:

#### Method 1: "It is said that..." (सबसे लोकप्रिय एवं सरल तरीका):
- **Active:** People say that honesty is the best policy.  
  $\rightarrow$ **It is said that honesty is the best policy.**
- **Active:** They believe that the ghost haunts the old palace.  
  $\rightarrow$ **It is believed that the ghost haunts the old palace.**
- **Active:** We know that hard work leads to success.  
  $\rightarrow$ **It is known that hard work leads to success.**
- **Active:** People expect that the prices will come down soon.  
  $\rightarrow$ **It is expected that the prices will come down soon.**

#### Method 2: Infinitive Construction (Subject-to-Subject Shift):
$$\mathbf{\text{Subordinate Subject} + \text{is/are said to be} + \dots}$$
- **Active:** People say that he is a spy.  
  $\rightarrow$ **He is said to be a spy.**
- **Active:** People consider that the king was generous.  
  $\rightarrow$ **The king is considered to have been generous.**

---

### 📕 Pattern 4.9: Prepositional Verbs / Phrasal Verbs (पूर्वसर्ग युक्त क्रियाएं)
यदि Active वाक्य की क्रिया के साथ कोई Preposition या Adverb Particle स्थायी रूप से जुड़ा हो (जैसे: *look after, laugh at, look down upon, care for, break into, send for*), तो Passive Voice बनाते समय उस Preposition को **क्रिया के तुरंत बाद बनाए रखना अनिवार्य है**, और उसके बाद 'by' भी लगेगा:

- *Active:* The girl laughed at the beggar.  
  $\rightarrow$ **The beggar was laughed at by the girl.** (❌ *was laughed by the girl* लिखना बहुत बड़ी गलती है!)
- *Active:* We must look after our elderly parents.  
  $\rightarrow$ **Our elderly parents must be looked after by us.**
- *Active:* The doctor sent for the compounder.  
  $\rightarrow$ **The compounder was sent for by the doctor.**
- *Active:* The police broke into the locked room.  
  $\rightarrow$ **The locked room was broken into by the police.**
- *Active:* You should not look down upon the poor.  
  $\rightarrow$ **The poor should not be looked down upon by you.**

---

### 📓 Pattern 4.10: Contextual Board Dialogues & Passage-Based Voice (संवाद आधारित वाच्य रूपान्तरण)
नवीनतम RBSE प्रश्नपत्रों में कभी-कभी दो व्यक्तियों के बीच संवाद (Dialogue) देकर रिक्त स्थान में Passive Voice भरने को कहा जाता है:

#### संवाद मॉडल 1:
- **Teacher:** Have you submitted the application form?
- **Student:** Yes sir, the application form ______________________ (submit) by me yesterday.
  * **Answer:** **was submitted** (चूंकि 'yesterday' दिया गया है, अतः Past Simple Passive का प्रयोग हुआ)।

#### संवाद मॉडल 2:
- **Mother:** Why is there so much noise in the kitchen?
- **Son:** Don't worry mother, tea ______________________ (prepare) by sister.
  * **Answer:** **is being prepared** (वर्तमान में कार्य चल रहा है $\rightarrow$ Present Continuous Passive)।

#### संवाद मॉडल 3:
- **Shopkeeper:** Can you pay the bill now?
- **Customer:** The bill ______________________ (already pay) through UPI by my brother.
  * **Answer:** **has already been paid** (Present Perfect Passive)।

## 🚫 5. Mistake Busters: शीर्ष 6 गलतियां जिनसे छात्र अंक गंवाते हैं

1. **Mistake 1:** Tense बदल देना!
   * याद रखें: **Voice में Tense कभी नहीं बदलता!** (Present tense Present ही रहेगा, Past Past ही रहेगा)। छात्र Direct/Indirect Speech के भ्रम में Present को Past में बदल देते हैं, जो कि 100% गलत है।
   * ❌ *Wrong:* He writes a letter. $\rightarrow$ A letter was written by him.
   * ✔️ *Correct:* A letter **is written** by him.
2. **Mistake 2:** Continuous Tenses में 'being' भूल जाना!
   * Continuous Tense की Passive में **is/am/are/was/were के बाद 'being'** लगाना अनिवार्य है:
   * ❌ *Wrong:* She is singing a song. $\rightarrow$ A song is sung by her.
   * ✔️ *Correct:* A song **is being sung** by her.
3. **Mistake 3:** 'Please' वाले वाक्यों में 'Please' को यथावत छोड़ देना:
   * ❌ *Wrong:* You are requested to please help me.
   * ✔️ *Correct:* You are requested to **help** me.
4. **Mistake 4:** 'Known' के साथ 'by' लगाना:
   * ❌ *Wrong:* He is known by me.
   * ✔️ *Correct:* He is **known to** me.
5. **Mistake 5:** Interrogative Passive में Helping Verb को Subject के बाद लगा देना:
   * ❌ *Wrong:* Was the glass broken by you? $\rightarrow$ Why the glass was broken by you?
   * ✔️ *Correct:* Why **was** the glass broken by you?
6. **Mistake 6:** 'Let' वाले वाक्यों में 'be' छोड़ देना:
   * ❌ *Wrong:* Let the door closed.
   * ✔️ *Correct:* Let the door **be closed**.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Active Structure                                       │ Passive Verb Formula                     │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Present Simple: V1 / does / do                         │ is / am / are + V3                       │
│ Present Continuous: is/am/are + V4                     │ is / am / are + BEING + V3               │
│ Present Perfect: has / have + V3                       │ has / have + BEEN + V3                   │
│ Past Simple: V2 / did + not                            │ was / were + V3                          │
│ Past Continuous: was / were + V4                       │ was / were + BEING + V3                  │
│ Past Perfect: had + V3                                 │ had + BEEN + V3                          │
│ Future Simple: will / shall + V1                       │ will / shall + BE + V3                   │
│ Modals: can / may / must / should + V1                 │ modal + BE + V3                          │
│ Imperative: Order / Command                            │ Let + Obj + BE + V3                      │
│ Imperative: Request (Please / Kindly)                  │ You are requested to + V1                │
│ Imperative: Advice / Moral Duty                        │ Obj + SHOULD BE + V3                     │
│ Known / Married                                        │ to (He is known TO me)                   │
│ Surprised / Shocked / Astonished                       │ at (I was surprised AT...)               │
│ Pleased / Satisfied / Filled                           │ with (Pleased WITH...)                   │
│ Contained / Interested                                 │ in (Contained IN...)                     │
│ Who wrote ...?                                         │ By whom was ... written?                 │
│ Unimportant Agents (someone, people, they, nobody)    │ Omit agent (Nobody -> add 'not')         │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. The police arrested two notorious thieves yesterday.
2. Please don't disturb me right now.
3. She gave him a useful dictionary on his birthday.
4. Who broke this antique flowerpot?
5. The mechanic has repaired our motorcycle.
6. We should protect the wild animals.
7. Shut all the classroom windows immediately.
8. His arrogant behaviour shocked the headmaster.
9. They are laying the foundation stone tomorrow.
10. Can you solve this puzzle?

---

### 📋 Test Marking Scheme & Solutions:
1. **Two notorious thieves were arrested yesterday.** [1 Mark] — Past Simple ($V_2$) $\rightarrow$ were + $V_3$; agent *by the police* omitted.
2. **You are requested not to disturb me right now.** [1 Mark] — Please imperative negative transformation.
3. **He was given a useful dictionary on his birthday by her.** [1 Mark] — (or: *A useful dictionary was given to him on his birthday by her.*)
4. **By whom was this antique flowerpot broken?** [1 Mark] — Who $\rightarrow$ By whom + was + Object + $V_3$?
5. **Our motorcycle has been repaired by the mechanic.** [1 Mark] — Present Perfect $\rightarrow$ has been + $V_3$.
6. **The wild animals should be protected by us.** [1 Mark] — Modal should $\rightarrow$ should be + $V_3$.
7. **Let all the classroom windows be shut immediately.** [1 Mark] — Imperative order $\rightarrow$ Let + Obj + be + $V_3$.
8. **The headmaster was shocked at his arrogant behaviour.** [1 Mark] — Shocked takes preposition *at*, not *by*.
9. **The foundation stone is being laid tomorrow.** [1 Mark] — Present Continuous $\rightarrow$ is being + $V_3$; agent *they* omitted.
10. **Can this puzzle be solved by you?** [1 Mark] — Interrogative modal $\rightarrow$ Can + Obj + be + $V_3$ + by + you?

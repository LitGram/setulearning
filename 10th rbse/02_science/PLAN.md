## 2026–27 syllabus alignment

The regular-secondary syllabus names the same 13 chapters and the same weights as this index: 6, 7, 5, 7, 8, 6, 7, 4, 8, 4, 7, 6, 5 (=80). Chapter scope notes and teaching sections were compared with the official outline on 14 October 2026. The listed chapter themes match: reactions/redox; acids and salts; metals and extraction; carbon compounds; life processes; control and coordination; reproduction; heredity; optics; eye/light; electricity; magnetic effects; and environment. The guide's Chapter 12 explicitly marks motor, electromagnetic induction, and generator as outside the syllabus's explicit board-core list. No chapter-weight or chapter-presence mismatch was found. This is a syllabus mapping, not an exhaustive claim-by-claim science review or answer-key audit.

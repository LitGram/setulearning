# RBSE Class 10 Books: Fact Check and Formatting Report

**Review dates:** 13–14 October 2026  
**Current syllabus reference:** RBSE Class 10 syllabus for session 2026–27 (board examination 2027).  
**Scope:** Six subject folders: Mathematics, Science, Social Science, Hindi, English, and Sanskrit.

## Audit scope and status

I checked 75 syllabus and exam-structure items across the six books against the current RBSE syllabus. This count covers scheme, marks, listed topics and chapter-weight rows, not every factual sentence in the books. I also compared two questions labelled as 2024 board questions against the official 2024 papers, reviewed the six indexes and tested their relative Markdown links.

This is not a complete claim-by-claim verification of every lesson, worked solution, MCQ, answer key, and historical claim in the six subject folders. The findings below state what was checked and what remains unresolved; they should not be described as an exhaustive scientific, historical, or literary peer review. High-risk unsupported score guarantees and unverified past-paper statistics were corrected or relabelled in selected files, but similar claims may remain elsewhere.

# RASSetu Book 3 graphics plan

## Scope and audit

Book 3 contains 44 app chapter records arranged as 22 paired lessons. At the start of planning, two distinct visuals are integrated in the national-movement lesson and repeated across its paired records: the selected-milestones timeline (`rassetu_b3_freedom_movement.webp`) and a representative Salt March scene (`rassetu_b3_salt_march_scene.webp`). The other 20 paired lessons have no chapter image references.

The series inventory has 12 Book 3 vector drafts. The freedom-movement timeline is already integrated; the other 11 are unplaced drafts. The contact sheet shows several drafts rendered as generic text boxes, so they are candidates for factual and visual review/redesign, not ready-to-place graphics. The Indus-sites draft also contains a disputed “dockyard” attribution and is excluded until that claim is resolved; the new locator should omit it. Existing text and MCQs remain source content and must not be edited to add these visuals.

**Recommended target: 24 distinct visuals** (the two already integrated plus 22 new), shared across paired chapter records where the lesson content is paired. The target is a coverage guide, not a requirement to fill every chapter. Aim for **20 precise vector maps/diagrams/timelines and four representative scenes** overall: one existing timeline and 19 new vectors, plus one existing scene and three new scenes. Do not reuse an existing visual where it would repeat the same concept.

### Representative scenes

Generate three scenes only if they add useful context beyond the exact diagrams: an archaeological reconstruction of an Indus urban street/water system (ch01–02), a representative South Indian temple-town/craft setting (ch11–12), and a non-documentary 1857-era civilian/sepoy scene (ch27–28). Captions must say **“Representative illustration — not a literal depiction”** / **“प्रतिनिधि चित्रण — वास्तविक दृश्य का हूबहू चित्र नहीं”**. Do not portray named historical people as exact likenesses. All other visuals should be precise diagrams, maps or timelines.

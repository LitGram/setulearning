# RASSetu remaining-books graphics plan

**Planning date:** 2026-10-08  
**Purpose:** Plan the next graphics work across the RASSetu catalog without changing lesson text, questions, answers, or packaging the app. This is a production plan; it does not add or generate graphics.

## 1. Catalog scope and exclusions

The app catalog contains twelve RASSetu books. Books 1–6 are the core six-volume series. Books 1, 2, 3 and 6 have complete topic coverage under their current plans; Books 4 and 5 have partial coverage. The supplementary catalog adds:

| App book | Catalog title | Plan decision |
|---:|---|---|
| 7 | RAS Mains: Answer Writing | **No new images or graphics.** Keep answer structure, diagrams and writing guidance as text-native study material. |
| 8 | RAS Paper IV: Language and Essay | **No new images or graphics.** This is a composition, language and essay-practice volume. |
| 9 | General Science for Competitive Exams | **Include.** Mechanism diagrams and scientific models are central to learning these topics. |
| 10 | Rajasthan Flagship Schemes | **Include selectively.** Prefer delivery pathways and sector frameworks over static lists of scheme amounts. |
| 11 | Rajasthan Economy and Budget | **Include.** Use dated maps and data graphics with definitions and official-source notes. |
| 12 | Administrative and General Hindi | **No new images or graphics.** Treat this as a writing, drafting and grammar volume; retain its useful text tables and worked formats. |

This interprets “answer-writing books” as Books 7 and 8, and also excludes Book 12 because its catalog content is administrative writing and Hindi grammar. Books 9–11 are not writing volumes. If the catalog later changes, revisit the scope before asset work.

## 10. Production, sources and verification gates

1. **Audit before drawing:** inspect each candidate chapter, existing tables/figures and current assets. Record exact chapter IDs and an anchor that occurs once. For Books 4–5, reuse the same asset in both paired lesson records; Books 9–11 use each catalog chapter ID as defined in their JSON.
2. **Choose form by learning need:** use precise vector diagrams for legal structure, processes, science and statistical graphics; use maps for spatially grounded Rajasthan topics; generated representative scenes only when context cannot be taught as clearly by a diagram. No generic decorative boxes.
3. **Date volatile evidence:** official schemes, fiscal figures, boundaries, institutions, projects, capacities and current indices require authoritative source verification at production time and an as-of/source date printed on the graphic and stored in the manifest. Prefer Rajasthan department orders/portals, Finance Department, Directorate of Economics and Statistics, CAG, official science agencies, standards bodies and primary legal texts as applicable.
4. **Use honest visual conventions:** label schematic maps **“Not to scale / पैमाने पर नहीं”**; distinguish estimate from actual, current from constant prices and proposed from operational projects. Label generated historical/context art as **“Representative illustration — not a literal depiction / प्रतिनिधि चित्रण — वास्तविक दृश्य का हूबहू चित्र नहीं.”**
5. **Preserve the source:** insert only image/caption/learning-note blocks. Keep all chapter prose, tables, questions, answer options and answer explanations unchanged. Record canonical pre-integration chapter and content/MCQ SHA-256 hashes.
6. **Manifest every placement:** include book/file ID, exact chapter ID, anchor, image filename, asset hash, source references, date, caption, explanatory note and (for generated images) prompt/source hash. Paired lessons receive a row for each chapter record.
7. **Review and verify:** inspect contact sheets and phone-width readability; check map labels, units, terms and date legibility; verify every referenced asset exists and hash-matches; compare chapter/text/MCQ hashes; confirm gallery and asset registry entries. No APK build/package step is included.

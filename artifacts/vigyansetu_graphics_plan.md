# VigyanSetu Science Series: Graphics Production Plan

## Scope and count

This plan specifies graphics for the six books without a completed graphics set: Physics I–II, Chemistry I–II, Environment & Ecology, and Science & Technology. The existing Biology I and II sets (46 and 64 placements; 110 total) are the style and workflow baseline and are not included in the new production count.

**Planned new work: 272 visual placements** across 140 chapters. A placement is one teaching figure or one coherent multi-panel plate; a plate with several panels still counts as one image. The book totals are Physics I 35, Physics II 51, Chemistry I 46, Chemistry II 38, Environment & Ecology 53, and Science & Technology 49. Existing suitable figures may be reused across chapters; reuse does not add a new placement or asset. Counts are editorial targets and can be reduced only when the chapter text lacks the concept or an accurate, legible figure cannot be made.

The titles below are shortened for scanability; chapter numbers follow the bundled VigyanSetu JSON order. Final insertion anchors must be selected from the current chapter text during implementation.

## Shared art direction and production rules

- Match the biology books: clean textbook illustration or editable schematic, white/light neutral canvas, high contrast, consistent colour key, concise English scientific labels, Hindi caption and Hindi learning note. Use bilingual labels only where the figure remains readable at phone width.
- Prefer diagrams that teach structure, sequence, comparison, scale, direction, or cause and effect. Avoid decorative images, photorealistic filler, and text-heavy poster layouts. One multi-panel plate may cover closely related cases; keep its count as one placement.
- Physics figures must show axes, vector directions, ray arrows, circuit polarity and sign conventions clearly. Chemistry figures must use correct symbols, charges, bonds, apparatus and reaction direction. Environment and technology maps must include a legend, scale/context where needed, and dated/current-source notes for changing facts.
- Keep safety content educational and verified against current authoritative guidance before illustration. Medical/first-aid visuals must show scope and avoid unsupported diagnosis or treatment claims. Sensitive defence topics stay at high-level educational overview.
- Review every figure at full resolution and at phone-reader width: check label endpoints, arrow direction, sequence, units, contrast, bilingual spelling and caption-to-text alignment. Preserve all original chapter prose and MCQs; insert each figure beside its teaching passage.
- Use SVG or other editable vector masters for plots, ray diagrams, circuits, reaction schemes, maps and flowcharts. Use raster artwork only when illustration materially improves understanding. Reuse an existing figure only when its labels and teaching purpose match the new passage.

## Implementation handoff

1. Re-read each current chapter and select a stable insertion anchor; do not rely on shortened titles as insertion keys.
2. Create a figure brief per placement from the table, specifying required labels, panel layout, scientific checks, caption and Hindi learning note.
3. Produce graphics in book batches, review each batch for scientific accuracy and phone readability, then insert without changing source lesson text or MCQs.
4. Maintain a manifest with book/chapter, anchor, asset filename, caption/note, source references, and source-content hash. Verify chapter order, all 230 planned placements, image decoding, zoom behavior and preservation of original content before packaging.
5. Keep original raster sources and editable masters outside the bundled asset directory; optimize final assets and report the added app download size.

## Assumptions

- The eight-book VigyanSetu catalog is the science series. Books 5–6 (Biology I–II) already have 110 placements and are a completed reference set.
- These six-book counts are planned image placements, not a promise that every placement requires a unique file; shared graphics are counted once per planned use in the placement total and tracked as reuse in the manifest.
- Use selective chapter-level coverage, with revision chapters receiving a compact map rather than a full illustration set.

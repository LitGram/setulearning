## Fractions

Fractions represent a part of a whole or collection of objects. When an object or number is divided into equal parts, each part is a fraction.

In a fraction, the number on the top is called the Numerator, and the number on the bottom is called the Denominator.

![fractions diagram](fractions_diagram.png)

Like fractions are fractions that have identical denominators. Adding like fractions is straightforward: we simply add the numerators together while keeping the denominator the same.

Q: What is 1/2 + 1/4?

B) 3/4
C) 1/6
D) 2/4

## Decimals

A decimal is another way of representing a fraction whose denominator is a power of 10 (such as 10, 100, 1000).

Decimals are widely used in everyday life, including currency calculations and precise measurements.

<!-- mcq -->
Q: What is 0.75 converted to its simplest fraction form?
A) 1/2
B) 2/3
C) 3/4
D) 4/5
Correct: C
<!-- /mcq -->
